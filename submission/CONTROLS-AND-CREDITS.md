# Plot Twist — Valley Exchange

**Theme:** Earn your living making coffee, then discover that a “plot twist” lets you physically rearrange the town — and uncover the original deeds in your dream car.

## Controls

- Mouse or touch: choose ingredients in ticket order, then follow the brewing prompt.
- Latte / iced mocha: hold the pour button; release at the gold line.
- Cappuccino: start, then tap on each of the three gold beats.
- Espresso: start, then stop the pressure needle inside the gold window.
- Keyboard: Tab moves focus; Enter activates controls. Space operates the focused brewing button (hold/release for pouring, tap for rhythm and pressure).
- Map: click/tap a parcel, or focus the map and use arrow keys.
- Escape: pause. Menus, conversations, results and background tabs pause both brewing and the economy.
- Use the next-move prompt to follow Mara, Dax and Nell's commissions. Their rewards are claimed once in order. Buildings develop nearby parcels; net sale profit includes construction and selling fees.
- The car costs **$3,900 cash**. Buying it plays the ending; Continue keeps your town.
- Sound is opt-in. Settings includes music/SFX levels and reduced motion. Progress saves locally when browser storage is available.

## Upload

Upload `plot-twist-jam.zip` as an HTML game. It includes a root `index.html` and relative asset paths. Use fullscreen launch, or a roomy desktop embed with scrollbars enabled. The layout also supports touch screens.

Official packaging reference: [itch.io HTML5 requirements](https://itch.io/docs/creators/html5).
